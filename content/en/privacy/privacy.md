#### Cookies
Cookies are small text files, stored on your computer by a web server, during your visit on a web page.
On the next visit of the web page, the stored cookies are provided to the web server by your web browser. Cookies are an external data storage of a web server.

Technically necessary cookies contain your settings, e.g. preferable language, content of a shopping basket, access credentials to a secured web page, or an information that you don't wish to be tracked (do-not-track-cookie).

Marketing cookies contain data concerning your user behavior on a web page. They're used to build your customer profile.

##### Scope
We use only the technically necessary cookies on our website.

##### Legal basis
Without the technically necessary cookies the functionality and usability of our website would suffer (art. 6 par. 1 lit. f GDPR).

##### Deleting
Session cookies are removed immediately after leaving out website (e.g. antiforgery cookie), the user credential cookies are removed after you sign-off. Other cookies are removed by their expiry date.

You can delete cookies in your web browser by clicking `Ctrl-Shift-Delete`.

It's also possible to completely disable using the cookies by your web browser, however disabling the technically necessary cookies is not recommended.
