Click `Mail Merge into Multiple Files`. A dialog will be open.

After opening the dialog, define filename pattern as a combination of free text and document fields.

A field can be selected by clicking the `+` button on the right. After showing the field list - select a field from the list and then:
- double click it or
- click the `Insert` button at the bottom
